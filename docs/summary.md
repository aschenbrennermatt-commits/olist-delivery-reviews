# Summary: Do Late Deliveries Hurt Review Scores?

## The question
Do late deliveries lower customer review scores on Olist, and by how much?

## What I found
- Late orders score **2 stars lower** on average (4.29 → 2.27). 54% of late orders get 1 star vs 7% of on-time orders.
- The gap holds after controlling for region, product category, price, shipping cost and number of sellers (−2.01 stars, 95% CI 1.97–2.05).
- The pattern suggests customers react to **missed promises**, not slow delivery: northern states wait longest but are rarely late, because they're promised long delivery windows.
- The Northeast has the highest late rates and pays the second-highest shipping costs.
- Orders from multiple sellers get ~1 star lower per extra seller, likely because separate parcels arrive late without being recorded.

## Recommendation
Because late deliveries cost about 2 stars per order, I recommend testing longer, more realistic delivery estimates in the states where Olist misses its promises most often, starting with the Northeast (AL, MA, SE). This is the most cost-effective option: it changes how the estimate is calculated rather than requiring new systems, and it reaches far more customers (6.7% of orders arrive late, compared with about 1% that come from multiple sellers). The risk is that longer promised dates could discourage some customers from buying, so it should run as a test first: extend estimates in a few states for a few months and compare both review scores and sales against similar states. As a smaller second step, Olist could track multi-seller orders per parcel so customers know when the rest of their order will arrive.

## What this can't prove
- This is observational data: it shows a strong link between lateness and low reviews, but not proof that lateness causes them. The test above would settle it.
- Multi-seller late deliveries are likely under-counted (~1% of orders).
- Almost every order has a review (99.2%), suggesting the public data may be pre-filtered, so results may not represent all customers.
